Privacy Policy - Kalen

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Developer: Angel Luis Santiago

1. Introduction

Kalen is a mobile language-learning application developed by Angel Luis Santiago. It is designed to assist users in learning English through vocabulary exercises, grammar quizzes, translation tools, speech and pronunciation practice (shadowing), educational audio/podcasts, and gamification features.

Kalen is built with an "offline-first" architecture. We store your learning data locally on your device, and we do not operate our own backend server or centralized user database. However, certain app features and integrated third-party services process technical or account-related information as described in this Policy.

By using Kalen, you acknowledge the data handling practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Kalen handles the following categories of data:

  • Google Account Information and Authentication Tokens: When you choose to sign in with Google, we access your Google email address and public display name to personalize your local profile. Authentication tokens issued by Google are handled exclusively on-device to maintain your session and authorize Google Drive backup access (via the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope). Kalen does not have a backend server; your tokens and credentials are never transmitted to or stored on any server owned or controlled by Kalen.
  • Learning Progress and Gamification: Learning metrics, completed levels (across the 12-level curriculum), review schedules, quiz scores, experience points (XP), streak counters, and achievements are stored directly in your device's local database.
  • Saved Words and User Preferences: Custom saved words, selected avatars, native language preferences, audio playback settings, and notification settings.
  • Voice and Microphone Input (Audio Data): When you engage in pronunciation practice or shadowing exercises, the microphone is accessed strictly in real time. As detailed in Section 6, voice input is processed entirely on your device and is never recorded, permanently saved, or transmitted to external servers.
  • Diagnostic and Crash Data: Stack traces, device hardware models, and session diagnostic information if Sentry reporting is enabled in the app build.
  • Advertising Data: Standard advertising identifiers and technical diagnostic information collected by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), subject to your consent choices.
  • External Media Requests: When playing audio episodes or podcasts from Voice of America (VOA Learning English) or LibriVox, your device connects directly to their third-party content servers, which may log standard HTTP request metadata (such as IP address).

3. How We Use Your Information

The information handled by Kalen is used exclusively to:

  • Deliver core educational functionality, track learning milestones, and manage the in-game economy.
  • Authenticate your identity securely via Google Sign-In without storing passwords.
  • Perform optional backup and restoration of your learning progress to your private Google Drive space.
  • Evaluate spoken pronunciation in real time during shadowing exercises.
  • Deliver optional local daily word notifications and streak reminders.
  • Diagnose application crashes and stability issues when crash reporting is enabled.
  • Serve advertisements in compliance with your privacy consent preferences.

4. Device Storage and Google Drive Synchronization

All your learning progress, saved vocabulary, and custom settings reside locally in a private SQLite database on your device.

If you enable Google Sign-In and cloud synchronization:

  • Kalen creates an unencrypted archive (kalen_backup_full.zip) containing your local database and preferences.
  • This file is uploaded directly from your device to your personal Google Drive account, restricted strictly to the hidden application data folder (appDataFolder via the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata OAuth scope). Kalen cannot access, view, or modify any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Kalen does not operate an intermediary server: the transfer occurs directly between your device and Google's servers.
  • Because Kalen does not add an additional encryption layer to the ZIP file beyond Google Drive's transport and storage security, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your Google account.
  • Cloud synchronization is entirely optional. If not enabled, all your learning data remains strictly on your device.

5. On-Device Translation (Google ML Kit)

For translating words, definitions, and examples, Kalen utilizes Google ML Kit's on-device translation engine.

  • Text translation is executed locally on your device. Your queried text is not sent to Kalen servers.
  • Language models required for translation are downloaded directly from Google servers when needed. Google may collect technical service telemetry and diagnostics in connection with model downloads in accordance with Google's ML Kit terms.

6. Microphone Access and Speech Recognition (Shadowing)

Kalen includes a "Shadowing" feature to help users practice their spoken pronunciation and speaking rhythm.

  • Runtime Permission: To use this feature, the App requests the android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO permission.
  • On-Device Processing: Voice data is processed locally in volatile memory using an embedded, on-device acoustic model (Vosk speech-recognition engine).
  • No Audio Retention or Cloud Transmission: Kalen does not record, retain, or store your voice audio files on device storage, nor does it transmit your audio or transcriptions to remote servers or third parties.
  • Real-Time Evaluation: Once the audio buffer is analyzed in memory to compare pronunciation against the target text, the temporary audio data is immediately discarded.

7. Third-Party Audio Streaming and Content

Kalen provides access to public and educational audio streams, including news features from Voice of America (VOA Learning English) and audiobooks from LibriVox.

  • When you stream an episode or audio clip, your device makes a direct HTTP/HTTPS request to the content provider’s hosting server.
  • The content host may receive standard network connection information (such as your device's IP address and user-agent). Kalen does not transmit any personal identifiers or profile data to these providers.

8. Advertising, Consent, and Analytics

Kalen integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

  • Consent Management: Before non-essential ad personalization occurs, the UMP consent dialog is presented to eligible users (such as users in the EEA/UK).
  • Ad Formats: The App may show banner, interstitial, native, or rewarded ads (for optional perks such as streak rescues).
  • Data Handled by AdMob: Depending on your device settings and consent, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID), approximate IP location, and ad interaction data.
  • For more details, consult Google’s Advertising Privacy Policy.

9. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics (Sentry)

Kalen may include Sentry to detect application crashes and optimize performance.

  • Sentry is disabled by default unless an active Data Source Name (DSN) is configured in the application build.
  • When active, crash reports transmit diagnostic technical details such as crash stack traces, device OS version, and memory state.
  • Kalen does not attach personal names, Google email addresses, voice recordings, or learning text to crash reports.

10. Android Permissions Used

  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO: Required solely for the shadowing/pronunciation practice feature. Audio is processed on-device and never stored or uploaded.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used on Android 13+ to send optional daily word widgets and streak reminders. These notifications are generated locally by your device and can be toggled off at any time in the app or system settings.
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for Google Sign-In, Google Drive backup sync, ML Kit translation model downloads, streaming external educational audio, and loading advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to verify internet availability before attempting synchronization or media streaming.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides haptic feedback during interactive quizzes.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: You can erase all locally stored data, progress, and preferences at any time by selecting the "Delete Account" / "Delete Data" option in the App Settings, or by clearing app storage / uninstalling Kalen.
  • Cloud Backups: The in-app deletion process attempts to permanently delete kalen_backup_full.zip from your Google Drive appDataFolder. You can also manually revoke Kalen's access or delete its hidden application data directly from your Google Drive Settings (Manage Apps).
  • Self-Service External Deletion: In compliance with Google Play requirements, if you have uninstalled the App or cannot access your device, you can use our dedicated, self-service deletion portal at https://anludev.github.io/kalen-account-deletion/ or request assistance at kalen.learn@gmail.com.
  • Third-Party Data: Deleting data within Kalen does not automatically erase diagnostic or advertising logs previously collected by independent third parties (such as Google or Sentry) pursuant to their respective retention policies.

12. Children's Privacy

Kalen is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a minor has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us at kalen.learn@gmail.com, and we will take immediate steps to remove the data.
